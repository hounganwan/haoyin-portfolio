Main Hero Image
01 / Project Overview

Reimagining musical participation through touch, light and rhythm.

Resonance is a wearable interaction system designed to help hearing-impaired seniors participate in shared musical experiences.

Instead of relying solely on sound, the system translates musical rhythm into tactile and visual feedback, creating another way to perceive and experience music.

Project Overview Image
02 / Research

Exploring music beyond the sense of hearing.

The research explores how hearing-impaired older adults experience music, rhythm and social participation, with particular attention to the sensory and emotional dimensions of musical experiences.

This perspective opens up opportunities to use other sensory channels to communicate musical information and create more inclusive forms of participation.

Research Image
03 / Key Question

How might wearable interaction translate musical rhythm into accessible multisensory experiences for hearing-impaired seniors?

04 / Design Opportunity

Turning rhythm into something that can be felt and seen.

Tactile

Translate musical rhythm into physical vibration so users can perceive changes in tempo and rhythm through touch.

Visual

Use light-based feedback to reinforce rhythmic information and create a shared visual experience.

05 / Design Concept

A wearable interface that transforms sound into multisensory rhythm.

Resonance translates selected characteristics of music into tactile and visual signals delivered through a wearable device.

The concept creates an alternative sensory pathway between music and the user, allowing rhythm to become a physical and visual experience rather than an exclusively auditory one.

Design Concept Image

07 / Wearable Design

Designing the wearable as an extension of the body.

The wearable form is designed to support comfortable everyday use while placing sensory feedback close to the user's body.

Physical form, material and interaction are considered together so that the device can become part of the user's musical experience rather than an intrusive technological object.

Wearable Design Image
08 / Interaction Design

Making rhythm perceptible through touch and light.

The interaction system explores how different rhythmic patterns can be represented through variations in vibration and light.

The goal is not to reproduce music literally, but to create an intuitive sensory relationship between rhythm and bodily perception.

Interaction Design Image

10 / Final Design

A wearable rhythm experience for more inclusive musical participation.

Resonance transforms musical rhythm into tactile and visual feedback, enabling hearing-impaired seniors to engage with music through alternative sensory pathways.

The final system positions the wearable not simply as an assistive device, but as a medium for participation, expression and shared experience.

Final Design Image
11 / Design Significance

Designing beyond the limitations of a single sense.

Resonance demonstrates how multisensory interaction can create alternative pathways for experiencing cultural and social activities.

By translating rhythm into touch and light, the project explores how inclusive interaction design can move beyond compensation toward new forms of participation and experience.
