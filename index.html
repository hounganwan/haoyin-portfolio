About

I am a service designer and design researcher exploring how design can contribute to healthier, more inclusive and socially meaningful futures.

My practice combines service design, interaction design, visual communication and research. I work across education, healthcare, ageing, technology and social-impact contexts.

Alongside practice, I teach and mentor emerging designers, helping them connect creative thinking with systems thinking, research and real-world impact.
