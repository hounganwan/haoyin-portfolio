03 / AGEING · CARE · INCLUSIVE DESIGN

Ageing & Inclusive Care

Exploring how design can make care experiences more inclusive, connected and dignified across personal and community contexts.

RoleDesigner / Researcher
Period2023
FocusAgeing · Care · Inclusion

01 / CONTEXT

Care is a system of relationships, not a single touchpoint.

This project explores ageing and care through a service-design lens, considering the relationships between older people, families, professionals, communities, spaces and services.

02 / DESIGN QUESTIONS

How can services support autonomy?

Design should enable people to participate in decisions about their own everyday lives rather than treating ageing only as a problem to be managed.

How can communities reduce isolation?

Social connection can be designed through places, activities, communication and shared support networks.

03 / METHOD

From individual needs to service ecosystems.

Use this page to document interviews, observations, stakeholder maps, journey maps, co-design activities, service blueprints and prototypes from the original project.

04 / REFLECTION

Inclusive ageing design starts by understanding people as active participants in their own lives, not passive recipients of care.
