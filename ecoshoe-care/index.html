01 / Project Overview

Reframing footwear care as an everyday circular design practice.

EcoShoe Care explores how design can extend the life of footwear by making maintenance, repair and care more accessible within everyday routines.

Rather than treating footwear as a disposable consumer product, the project investigates the relationship between material durability, user behaviour and long-term product care.

The concept combines wearable technology, biomaterial exploration and service-oriented thinking to create a more continuous relationship between people and the products they use.

02 / Research

From disposable footwear to longer-lasting relationships with products.

The environmental impact of footwear extends beyond manufacturing. Short product lifecycles, material degradation and limited access to convenient maintenance can all contribute to premature disposal.

This project investigates how design might intervene before a product reaches the point of replacement.

Instead of asking how to make another pair of shoes, EcoShoe Care asks how we might help people maintain, understand and care for the products they already own.

03 / Key Question

How might wearable technology and biomaterials encourage people to care for footwear and extend its useful life?

04 / Design Opportunity

Making product care visible, understandable and actionable.

Product maintenance is often invisible until something goes wrong. Users may notice discomfort, damage or deterioration only after the condition of a product has already declined.

EcoShoe Care explores the possibility of creating a more proactive relationship between the user and their footwear through sensing, material feedback and accessible care interventions.

The design opportunity lies in transforming maintenance from an occasional repair activity into a continuous and understandable part of everyday use.

05 / Design Concept

A wearable care system embedded within everyday footwear.

EcoShoe Care proposes a system in which footwear itself becomes an interface for understanding condition, usage and maintenance.

Wearable sensing can provide information about patterns of use and potential areas of stress, while biomaterial components introduce a more sustainable approach to physical repair and replacement.

Together, these elements create a system that connects digital awareness with tangible acts of care.

06 / System

Connecting sensing, material intervention and everyday behaviour.

EcoShoe Care brings together three layers of interaction: sensing, feedback and physical care.

01 / Sense

Understand

Wearable sensing explores how footwear usage and physical conditions can become visible to the user.

02 / Respond

Care

Timely feedback encourages users to respond to deterioration before it becomes a reason for replacement.

03 / Sustain

Extend

Biomaterial-based interventions explore how products can be repaired, refreshed and kept in use for longer.

07 / Biomaterial Exploration

Exploring materials as part of the care system.

Biomaterial exploration investigates how sustainable materials can become active components of product maintenance rather than simply alternatives to conventional materials.

The project considers material behaviour, durability and repairability as part of a broader circular design strategy.

08 / Product Design

Designing care into the physical product experience.

The product development explores how sensing components, footwear structure and material interventions can work together without disrupting the everyday experience of wearing the shoe.

The design aims to balance technological functionality with comfort, simplicity and the familiarity of existing footwear behaviours.

09 / Prototype

Testing the relationship between technology, material and use.

Prototyping was used to investigate how the proposed system could operate within the physical constraints of footwear.

Iterative development helped explore form, material behaviour, interaction and the practical requirements of maintaining a wearable product over time.

10 / Service System

Moving from product ownership toward product stewardship.

EcoShoe Care extends beyond the physical shoe to consider the wider ecosystem required to support long-term product care.

Maintenance guidance, repair interventions and material replacement can become part of a more continuous service relationship between users, products and care providers.

This shifts the role of design from delivering a finished product toward supporting the entire lifecycle of that product.

11 / Final Design

Designing footwear as something to care for, not simply consume.

EcoShoe Care brings together wearable technology, biomaterials and service design to explore a more circular relationship between people and footwear.

By making product condition more visible and care interventions more accessible, the project proposes a shift from replacement-oriented consumption toward longer-term product stewardship.

12 / Design Significance

Extending sustainability from materials to everyday behaviour.

EcoShoe Care suggests that circular design is not only a question of choosing better materials. It also requires new relationships between people and the products they use.

By combining material innovation with wearable interaction and service thinking, the project explores how design can make sustainable behaviour more tangible, timely and embedded within everyday life.
