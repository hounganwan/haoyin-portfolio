EcoShoe Care

A Foot Health System for Agricultural Workers

Project Leader

Haoyin Yin · MA Service Design, Royal College of Art

Team Member

Bohan Yu · BFA Fashion and Accessories Design, Sichuan University

Year

2024

Discipline

Service Design · Footwear · Healthcare

Focus

Farmers · Foot Health · Traditional Medicine · Smart Wearables

Project Overview

EcoShoe Care is a product-service system designed to address the foot health problems experienced by farmers during long-term agricultural work.

The project explores how footwear, digital technology and therapeutic approaches can work together to support farmers' feet in demanding working environments.

The User

Farmers spend extended periods standing, walking and working on uneven agricultural terrain. Their footwear therefore becomes an important interface between the body and the working environment.

The Challenge

Existing footwear solutions often focus on basic protection and durability, while the longer-term needs of farmers' foot health, comfort and recovery remain under-addressed.

Context & Field Investigation

The research began by looking at farmers' working environments, daily routines and the physical conditions that influence foot health.

Field investigation, working environments and farmer interviews

What We Learned

Field observations and interviews revealed that foot care cannot be separated from the realities of agricultural work.

01

Working Environment

Farmers work across changing terrain and weather conditions, creating continuous physical demands on the feet.

02

Everyday Foot Problems

Prolonged agricultural work can create discomfort and recurring foot-related problems that are difficult to address through conventional footwear alone.

03

Beyond Protection

The opportunity was not simply to make a better shoe, but to connect prevention, monitoring and foot care within one system.

Key Question

How might we create a footwear system that supports farmers not only while they work, but also before and after work, helping them monitor and care for their feet?

Solution Exploration

The solution exploration moved from the immediate problem of footwear towards a broader system combining physical protection, therapeutic care and digital support.

01

Footwear

A protective and comfortable shoe designed around the physical requirements of agricultural work.

02

Therapeutic Care

Traditional Chinese medicine and herbal treatment were explored as part of a more holistic approach to foot care.

03

Digital Support

A companion digital interface extends the footwear experience by providing information related to the user's foot and working conditions.

Traditional Knowledge & Herbal Exploration

Traditional Chinese medicine became an important direction in exploring how foot care could move beyond mechanical protection towards prevention and therapeutic support.

Five Elements

The project investigated the principles of traditional Chinese medicine, including the Five Elements system, as a framework for understanding therapeutic approaches to foot health.

Herbal Materials

Different natural and herbal materials were explored according to their potential role in supporting foot treatment and everyday recovery.

Biomaterial Exploration

Material experimentation investigated how natural and herbal-based materials could become part of the physical treatment and footwear system.

The experiments explored material formation, surface characteristics and potential antibacterial properties, gradually narrowing the material possibilities for the final product.

Biomaterial exploration and experimental material development

Experimental Results

Material testing was used to evaluate the feasibility of the selected materials for integration into a wearable footwear system.

01

Antibacterial Properties

Experimental testing investigated whether the selected material could provide useful antibacterial performance for a foot-worn product.

02

Flexibility

Material flexibility was evaluated to understand whether the material could withstand movement and repeated use.

03

Product Integration

The experimental findings informed decisions about where and how the materials could be incorporated into the footwear structure.

Advanced Footwear Design

The design evolved from material experiments into a footwear system specifically considered around farmers' working movements, foot protection and therapeutic care.

Wearable Structure

The footwear architecture was developed to combine comfort, protection and replaceable functional components, allowing the shoe to become more than a conventional footwear product.

Therapeutic Components

The internal structure explores how therapeutic and supportive elements can be incorporated into the relationship between the foot and the shoe.

Product Design & Prototype

The final product combines the explored material system, footwear structure and smart components into a wearable prototype for agricultural workers.

The design includes a modular internal structure, allowing different components to be considered independently while maintaining the overall function of the footwear.

Product development, structural exploration and prototype

Smart Footwear System

EcoShoe Care extends the physical shoe into a connected system that can provide information about the user's foot and working conditions.

01

Sensing

Embedded sensing components explore how information related to the user's foot and environment can be captured during everyday use.

02

Monitoring

The system provides a way to monitor conditions such as temperature and use-related information.

03

Feedback

Digital feedback helps connect information collected from the footwear with the user's ongoing foot-care experience.

Companion App

A companion mobile interface extends the physical footwear into an ongoing foot-care experience.

Environmental Feedback

The interface visualises information collected by the footwear, helping users understand the conditions surrounding their feet during work.

Everyday Care

Rather than treating the shoe as an isolated product, the interface supports a continuous relationship between monitoring, awareness and foot care.

Final Design

EcoShoe Care brings together footwear, sensing technology, digital interaction and therapeutic knowledge into a holistic foot-care system for farmers.

EcoShoe Care final design

From Shoe to Care System

The project ultimately reframes the shoe as an interface connecting the farmer, the working environment, health information and therapeutic care.

01

During Work

The footwear provides physical support while collecting information from the user's everyday working environment.

02

After Work

Foot-care and therapeutic approaches extend the system beyond the moment of wearing the shoe.

03

Continuous Care

The digital interface connects physical footwear with longer-term awareness and care.

Design Significance

EcoShoe Care explores how traditional knowledge and contemporary technology can be brought together through service design to address an overlooked healthcare need.

Designing with Context

Instead of designing footwear in isolation, the project begins with the realities of agricultural work and the everyday experiences of farmers.

Connecting Tradition & Technology

Traditional therapeutic knowledge, biomaterial experimentation and digital technology are brought together as complementary parts of one care system.

Reflection

The project shifted my understanding of footwear from a physical product into a potential health interface.

By working across field research, material experimentation, traditional Chinese medicine, product design and digital interaction, EcoShoe Care demonstrates how service design can connect different forms of knowledge around a specific human need.

The project also opened up a broader question for future development: how can technology support health without replacing the cultural and contextual knowledge already embedded in local care practices?

From protective footwear to a connected system for everyday foot care.

EcoShoe Care explores how footwear can become a bridge between agricultural work, health monitoring, therapeutic knowledge and everyday care.
