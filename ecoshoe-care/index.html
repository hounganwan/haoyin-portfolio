EcoShoe Care

Wearable Footwear & Biomaterial System

Project Leader

Haoyin Yin · MA Service Design, Royal College of Art

Team Member

Bohan Yu · BFA Fashion and Accessories Design, Sichuan University

Year

2024

Discipline

Wearable · Biomaterials · Product-Service Design

Focus

Sustainability · Footwear · Everyday Care

Project Overview

EcoShoe Care explores how footwear can become a more sustainable and caring everyday product through the combination of wearable design, biomaterials and new approaches to product care.

The Challenge

Everyday footwear is closely connected to movement, comfort and personal wellbeing, yet conventional footwear systems often generate material waste and provide limited opportunities for long-term care and reuse.

The Opportunity

The project investigates how material innovation and footwear design can work together to create a more sustainable and meaningful relationship between people and the products they use every day.

Research

The research examined the relationship between footwear, everyday use, material choices and product longevity.

EcoShoe Care research and exploration

Key Question

How might we rethink footwear as a cared-for, adaptable and more sustainable everyday system rather than a disposable product?

Design Opportunity

01

Material

Explore alternative material possibilities that can support a more sustainable footwear lifecycle.

02

Wearable

Consider comfort, movement and the relationship between material and the human body.

03

Care

Shift the focus from short-term consumption towards maintenance, longevity and continued use.

Design Concept

EcoShoe Care brings together material exploration and footwear design to investigate a more sustainable model of everyday wearable products.

Biomaterial Exploration

Material experimentation became an important part of the design process, investigating how alternative materials could influence the physical character, tactility and sustainability of the shoe.

EcoShoe Care biomaterial exploration

Product Design

The material exploration informed the development of the footwear form, considering how material behaviour and wearable comfort could be integrated within the product.

EcoShoe Care product design

Service System

Beyond the physical shoe, EcoShoe Care considers product care as part of the overall experience, encouraging users to maintain and extend the life of their footwear.

01

Use

Everyday interaction between the wearer and the footwear.

02

Care

Maintenance becomes an integrated part of product ownership.

03

Longevity

Extending the useful life of the product through continued care.

Final Design

The final outcome brings together the explored material language, footwear form and care-oriented design approach into a cohesive EcoShoe Care system.

EcoShoe Care final design

Design Significance

EcoShoe Care demonstrates how product design can connect material innovation with everyday care and sustainability, opening up new possibilities for more responsible wearable product systems.

Material as Experience

Biomaterial exploration is treated not only as a technical investigation, but also as a way of shaping the tactile and emotional experience of the product.

Care as Design

The project reframes sustainability through the relationship between people, products and long-term everyday care.

Recognition

EcoShoe Care was developed as part of Haoyin Yin's exploration of wearable design, sustainability and service-oriented product systems.

Designing for care, material responsibility and longer product lives.

EcoShoe Care explores how a familiar everyday object can become an opportunity to rethink material use, product longevity and sustainable interaction.
