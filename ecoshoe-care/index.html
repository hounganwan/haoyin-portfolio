EcoShoe Care

Designing a Circular Footwear Care System Through Wearable Technology and Biomaterials

Wearable · Biomaterials · Product-Service Design

Sustainability · Footwear · Everyday Care

Project Overview

Rethinking footwear care as part of a circular everyday system.

EcoShoe Care explores how footwear can be designed not only for use, but also for long-term care, maintenance and extended life. The project brings together wearable technology, biomaterials and service design to rethink the relationship between people and the products they use every day.

Rather than treating worn footwear as waste at the end of its lifecycle, EcoShoe Care proposes a system that encourages people to understand the condition of their shoes, care for them through everyday interventions and ultimately extend their useful life.

01 / Research

Understanding the hidden lifecycle of footwear.

Footwear is exposed to continuous physical stress, moisture, friction and environmental conditions throughout everyday use. However, maintenance is often reactive rather than proactive, and many people replace shoes when they are visibly worn rather than considering how their condition could be monitored and improved.

The research therefore investigated the everyday behaviours, material conditions and opportunities surrounding footwear care, looking beyond the shoe itself toward the wider relationship between user, product and maintenance service.

02 / Key Question

How might wearable technology and biomaterials encourage people to care for footwear and extend its useful life?

03 / Design Opportunity

From replacement to care.

The opportunity lies in making footwear care more visible, understandable and engaging. Instead of relying on users to recognise deterioration themselves, the system can create a feedback loop between sensing, material intervention and everyday behaviour.

This shifts the design challenge from creating another disposable product toward creating an ecosystem that supports longer use and more responsible consumption.

04 / Design Concept

A footwear care system connecting sensing, material and service.

EcoShoe Care combines three interconnected layers: understanding the condition of footwear, providing appropriate care interventions, and extending the product's useful life.

01

Understand

Make everyday footwear conditions more visible through wearable sensing and feedback.

02

Care

Provide material and maintenance interventions that respond to specific footwear conditions.

03

Extend

Encourage longer product use by integrating care into everyday routines and services.

05 / Biomaterial Exploration

Materials designed for a second life.

Biomaterial exploration investigates how renewable and biodegradable materials could become part of footwear care rather than simply part of footwear production.

The material system is considered as an active component of maintenance, creating opportunities for repair, replacement and regeneration while reducing dependence on disposable maintenance products.

06 / Product Design

Bringing care into the everyday footwear experience.

The product design translates the broader care system into a tangible wearable and footwear interaction. The design considers how sensing, feedback and material intervention can become part of the user's normal routine without adding unnecessary complexity.

The physical form is developed around comfort, accessibility and the practical realities of everyday footwear use.

07 / Prototype

Testing the relationship between technology, material and behaviour.

Prototyping was used to explore the physical interaction, material qualities and potential service touchpoints of the EcoShoe Care system.

08 / Service System

Extending footwear care beyond the product.

EcoShoe Care is conceived as a product-service system rather than a standalone wearable product. The service layer connects users with information, maintenance interventions and opportunities to repair or renew their footwear.

01

Awareness

Help users understand when and why footwear requires care.

02

Maintenance

Connect users with appropriate care and material solutions.

03

Extension

Support longer product lifecycles and more responsible consumption.

09 / Final Design

A circular approach to everyday footwear care.

The final EcoShoe Care proposal brings together wearable interaction, biomaterial exploration, product design and service thinking into a unified footwear care ecosystem.

Rather than focusing solely on the production of a new shoe, the project asks how design can support the continued use, maintenance and renewal of products that already exist.

10 / Design Significance

Designing for longer relationships with products.

EcoShoe Care explores how sustainability can move beyond material substitution and become part of everyday product relationships.

By combining wearable technology, biomaterials and service design, the project proposes a more active role for users in maintaining and extending the life of their possessions.
