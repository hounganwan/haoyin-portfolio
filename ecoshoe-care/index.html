EcoShoe Care

Designing a Circular Footwear Care System Through Wearable Technology and Biomaterials

Haoyin Yin, MA Service Design, Royal College of Art

Team Member

Year

2024

Discipline

Wearable · Biomaterials · Product-Service Design

Focus

Sustainability · Footwear · Everyday Care

Project Overview

Rethinking footwear care through a circular design approach.

EcoShoe Care explores how footwear can be designed as part of a longer and more sustainable relationship between people, products and materials.

Instead of treating footwear as a disposable object that eventually becomes waste, the project investigates how design can encourage people to better understand, maintain and extend the life of their shoes.

The project combines wearable technology, biomaterial exploration and product-service design to create a more proactive approach to everyday footwear care.

01 / Research

Understanding how footwear is used, worn and discarded.

Footwear is continuously exposed to friction, moisture, pressure and environmental conditions. Over time, these factors gradually affect the material and performance of the shoe.

However, footwear care is often reactive. People tend to respond only when damage becomes visible, while maintenance and repair are frequently separated from the everyday experience of using the product.

The research therefore looked at footwear not as a single finished product, but as part of an ongoing lifecycle involving use, maintenance, repair and eventual replacement.

02 / Key Question

How might wearable technology and biomaterials encourage people to care for footwear and extend its useful life?

03 / Design Opportunity

From replacement to care.

The project identifies an opportunity to make footwear care more visible and easier to integrate into everyday behaviour.

Rather than asking users to replace footwear when it reaches the end of its perceived life, the system encourages earlier intervention through sensing, material care and maintenance.

This reframes sustainability from simply choosing better materials to designing better relationships with existing products.

04 / Design Concept

A circular footwear care system connecting technology, material and behaviour.

EcoShoe Care proposes a system in which footwear condition, maintenance and material intervention are connected rather than treated as separate activities.

01

Understand

Help users recognise the condition and changing needs of their footwear.

02

Care

Introduce appropriate maintenance and material interventions into everyday routines.

03

Extend

Support longer use and reduce unnecessary replacement through continuous care.

05 / Biomaterial Exploration

Exploring materials as part of the care cycle.

Biomaterial exploration investigates how alternative materials can contribute to a more circular footwear lifecycle.

Instead of considering material only as something used to manufacture the shoe, the project explores how material qualities can support repair, maintenance and renewal.

This creates a connection between the physical material system and the wider ambition of extending product life.

06 / Product Design

Bringing footwear care into the everyday experience.

The product design translates the wider circular care concept into a tangible footwear experience.

Wearable technology is considered not simply as a technological feature, but as a means of creating a more visible relationship between the user and the condition of the product.

The physical design focuses on usability, comfort and the integration of care into existing routines.

07 / Service System

Making care part of the product lifecycle.

EcoShoe Care extends beyond the physical footwear product to consider the supporting service ecosystem around maintenance and repair.

The system connects information, product use and care interventions, creating opportunities for users to make more informed decisions about when and how their footwear should be maintained.

In this way, sustainability becomes an ongoing service relationship rather than a one-time purchasing decision.

01

Awareness

Make the condition of footwear easier to understand.

02

Maintenance

Encourage timely care before damage becomes irreversible.

03

Extension

Support longer and more meaningful product lifecycles.

08 / Final Design

Designing a longer relationship between people and footwear.

The final EcoShoe Care proposal brings together wearable interaction, biomaterial exploration and product-service thinking into a circular footwear care system.

The project shifts the focus from designing products for replacement toward designing systems that support continued use, maintenance and renewal.

09 / Design Significance

Designing for longer product relationships.

EcoShoe Care explores sustainability as a behavioural and systemic design challenge rather than only a material challenge.

By connecting wearable technology, biomaterials and service design, the project proposes a more active role for users in maintaining and extending the life of everyday products.
