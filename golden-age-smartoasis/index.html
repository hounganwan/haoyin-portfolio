Golden Age Smartoasis

Design of an Age-Friendly Smart Community

Project Overview

Golden Age Smartoasis is a service design project that explores how an age-friendly smart community can alleviate loneliness and strengthen intergenerational connections.

Rather than treating ageing as an isolated healthcare issue, the project approaches loneliness as a social and community challenge. It connects a modular physical product, a digital platform and community space into one integrated ecosystem.

The Core Problem

As populations age, many older adults experience reduced companionship, weaker social connections and increasing feelings of loneliness.

The Design Approach

The project uses intergenerational integration as a foundation, creating opportunities for older and younger people to interact through shared community activities.

Desk Research

The research began by examining the changing global ageing population, the mental health implications of ageing and existing age-friendly community and smart-care solutions.

Golden Age Smartoasis proposes a new service system centred on seniors within the community, connecting product, application and community space.

01

AGEJOY App

A digital platform for communication, community activities, product exchange and intergenerational participation.

02

03

Community Space

A physical environment designed to encourage interaction between older adults, younger people and neighbours.

UnionCube — Modular Community Product

UnionCube is a modular physical product designed to support flexible community activities and intergenerational exchange.

The product evolved through multiple rounds of prototyping. Early tests revealed issues around assembly, usability and elderly-friendly design, which informed subsequent iterations.

UnionCube prototype development and first design iterations

From Prototype to Modular System

Further development refined UnionCube around modularity, magnetic connections, ergonomic dimensions and a more elderly-friendly visual language.

Advanced UnionCube design, material structure and modular system

01

Modular

Modules can be assembled and disassembled to support different community scenarios.

02

Magnetic

Magnetic connections simplify joining and reconfiguration compared with more complex assembly structures.

03

Elderly-Friendly

Rounded forms, accessible dimensions and clearer visual differentiation respond to elderly users' needs.

AGEJOY App

AGEJOY extends the community experience into a digital platform where older and younger users can communicate, participate and exchange.

AGEJOY app information architecture and early interface design

Elderly-Friendly Interface

User testing showed that older users preferred larger text, clearer images and simpler page structures, leading to an elderly-friendly version of the interface.

AGEJOY final interface and elderly-friendly interaction design

Research Methods & Validation

The final service concept was evaluated through field testing and a pre-test / post-test study using the UCLA Loneliness Scale, Third Edition.

Field testing, research methodology and validation results

Pre-test
50.37 ± 8.62

Extreme loneliness level recorded before the intervention.

Post-test
38.21 ± 7.14

Intermediate level recorded after the intervention, representing a 24.26% reduction.

Design Significance

Golden Age Smartoasis moves beyond single-modal design by integrating product, app and community space into one interconnected service ecosystem.

From Product to Ecosystem

The project demonstrates how a modular physical product, digital service and spatial environment can work together to support a continuous community experience.

From Loneliness to Connection

Instead of addressing loneliness only through individual services, the project creates opportunities for social participation, neighbour interaction and intergenerational exchange.

Reflection

The project changed my understanding of ageing from a primarily functional problem into a question of social connection, participation and belonging.

Through the development of UnionCube, AGEJOY and the community space, the project explored how product, digital and spatial interventions can work together rather than operate as isolated design outcomes.

The research and validation process also reinforced the importance of designing with the social realities of older adults, rather than assuming that providing facilities or technology alone will resolve loneliness.

Designing connections, not just spaces, products or interfaces.

Golden Age Smartoasis explores how service design can connect physical products, digital interaction and community space to create more socially connected ageing experiences.
