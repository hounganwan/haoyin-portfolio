01 / Project Overview

Designing more inclusive care experiences for ageing populations.

Golden Age SmartOasis explores how design can create more inclusive, supportive and meaningful care experiences for older adults.

The project looks beyond individual healthcare interventions and considers the wider ecosystem of ageing, care, everyday life and community support.

Project Overview Image
02 / Research

Understanding ageing as an experience, not simply a healthcare issue.

The research investigates the everyday experiences surrounding ageing and care, mapping relationships between older adults, families, caregivers, healthcare systems and community environments.

Rather than focusing exclusively on medical needs, the project considers emotional wellbeing, independence, social connection and the ability to participate in everyday life.

Research Image
03 / Key Question

How might we create more inclusive care experiences that support older adults' independence, wellbeing and connection with others?

04 / Design Opportunity

From care as a service to care as an everyday experience.

Personal

Supporting individual wellbeing, autonomy and confidence in everyday activities.

Social

Strengthening relationships between older adults, families, caregivers and wider communities.

05 / Design Concept

Creating a supportive environment where ageing, care and everyday life can coexist.

The concept explores an integrated care experience that connects personal needs with community resources and social interaction.

Instead of treating older adults as passive recipients of care, the design positions them as active participants within a broader care ecosystem.

07 / Experience Design

Designing touchpoints across the ageing journey.

The service experience is structured around different moments of everyday life, creating opportunities for support before, during and after care interactions.

Stage 01

Discover

Identify needs, interests and opportunities for participation.

Stage 02

Connect

Connect individuals with people, resources and community services.

Stage 03

Participate

Encourage meaningful engagement through everyday activities and social experiences.

Stage 04

Sustain

Build long-term relationships and continuity of support.

Experience Design Image
08 / Prototype

From service ideas to tangible care experiences.

Prototyping was used to explore how different service touchpoints, interactions and environments could work together as one coherent experience.

Prototype Image
09 / Final Design

A more connected approach to inclusive ageing.

Golden Age SmartOasis brings together personal wellbeing, community participation and care support into a more connected service experience.

The final proposal demonstrates how service design can shift the focus from isolated care interventions toward longer-term, inclusive relationships between people and their environments.

Final Design Image
10 / Design Significance

Designing for ageing means designing for participation, dignity and connection.

The project demonstrates the potential of service design to reframe ageing not simply as a problem of healthcare provision, but as a complex social and everyday experience.

By connecting personal needs with wider care networks and community environments, inclusive design can help create more sustainable and meaningful experiences of ageing.
