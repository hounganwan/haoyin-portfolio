Golden Age SmartOasis

Design of an Age-Friendly Smart Community

Focus

Project Overview

Golden Age SmartOasis is an age-friendly smart community ecosystem designed to address loneliness among older adults through product, digital service and spatial intervention.

Rather than treating ageing as an isolated healthcare problem, the project explores how community participation and intergenerational interaction can create stronger social connections for older adults.

The Challenge

Ageing can be accompanied by reduced companionship, weaker social connections and limited opportunities for meaningful interaction with other generations.

The Opportunity

The project investigates how a connected community ecosystem can create more opportunities for older and younger people to meet, participate and exchange.

Design Concept

Golden Age SmartOasis moves beyond single-modal design by integrating a physical product, a digital platform and community space into one connected ecosystem.

01

UnionCube

A modular physical product designed to support community activities, exchange and flexible use of shared spaces.

02

AGEJOY

A digital platform connecting older and younger users through communication, activities and community exchange.

03

Smart Community Space

A physical environment where digital connections become opportunities for face-to-face participation.

UnionCube

UnionCube is a modular physical product designed to facilitate community interaction and flexible participation.

The product was developed through multiple rounds of prototyping, exploring its form, assembly, usability and suitability for older users.

UnionCube product development and prototype iterations

The later iterations refined UnionCube around modularity, magnetic connection, ergonomic considerations and elderly-friendly interaction.

03

Interface iterations explored how AGEJOY could become easier for older adults to understand and navigate.

The design process considered larger visual elements, clearer information hierarchy and simplified interaction structures to improve accessibility for older users.

Validation

The service concept was evaluated through user testing and a pre-test / post-test study using the UCLA Loneliness Scale, Third Edition.

Validation process, testing methodology and evaluation results

Pre-Test
50.37 ± 8.62

UCLA Loneliness Scale score before the intervention.

Post-Test
38.21 ± 7.14

Score after the intervention, representing a 24.26% reduction.

Design Significance

Golden Age SmartOasis explores how service design can move beyond single-modal interventions by integrating product, digital service and spatial experience into one ecosystem.

From Product to Ecosystem

UnionCube, AGEJOY and the community space are designed as interconnected parts of one service rather than independent outputs.

From Loneliness to Participation

Instead of addressing loneliness only through individual care, the project creates opportunities for participation, neighbour interaction and intergenerational exchange.

Reflection

This project shifted my understanding of ageing from a primarily functional problem towards a broader question of social connection, participation and belonging.

Working across service design, product design, digital interaction and spatial experience demonstrated how different design modalities can work together around one social challenge.

More importantly, the project highlighted that designing for older adults should not simply mean making existing products easier to use. It also means creating meaningful opportunities for people to connect with one another and participate in their communities.

Designing connections, not just products, interfaces or spaces.

Golden Age SmartOasis explores how product, digital service and community space can work together to create more connected and participatory ageing experiences.
